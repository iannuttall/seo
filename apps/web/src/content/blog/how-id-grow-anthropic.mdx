[Claude](https://claude.com/) is the AI assistant and model family built by Anthropic. It makes money from subscriptions (Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise plans) and from the Claude API that developers pay for by the token.

Earlier this year Anthropic advertised for an [SEO Lead](https://web.archive.org/web/20260623120558/https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5102291008) to "own organic search strategy and technical SEO infrastructure across Anthropic's web properties", naming claude.ai, docs.anthropic.com and anthropic.com. The listing has since closed. This post is what I'd do in that job, using only what anyone can see from outside: public pages, search data and AI answers.

Key findings, from data pulled on 6 November 2026:

- ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Google's AI Overviews named Claude in 56 of 60 answers to 20 buyer questions, and in 54 of 60 when I asked again. 17 and then 18 of the 60 linked a Claude or Anthropic page, and only 3 and then 5 of Google's 40 did.
- About 0.6% (19,481 of 3,370,973) of the estimated US Google visits to claude.ai, claude.com and anthropic.com come from searches that don't name Claude or Anthropic.
- No Claude or Anthropic page is in Google's live results for "chatgpt alternative" (22,200 US searches a month, difficulty 1; 99 results) or "ai assistant" (301,000, difficulty 25; 43 results).
- For "claude vs chatgpt" (49,500 searches a month), 5 of the 13 sources in Google's AI Overview are YouTube videos. None is from Anthropic.
- On claude.com/pricing, the title, canonical and hreflang tags arrive after the head for Googlebot, 1.3 MB into the page.
- 72 of the 300 most-linked old support.anthropic.com addresses now end in a 404, and 159 of the 300 most-linked docs.anthropic.com addresses pass through a temporary redirect.

Claude doesn't have a brand problem. I asked ChatGPT search, Google AI Mode and Google's AI Overviews 20 questions a buyer might ask ("what is the best AI for writing?", "what are the best ChatGPT alternatives?", "which AI assistant is best for lawyers?"), through `seo research ai_answers` with DataForSEO on 6 November 2026. Claude was named in 56 of the 60 answers. I asked the same 20 questions again later that day, and it was named in 54. Answers vary from one run to the next, so I give both.

The gap is the link. 17 of the 60 answers linked a Claude or Anthropic page in the first run, and 18 in the second. ChatGPT did most of the linking (13 of its 20 answers in the second run). Google's two engines linked one in 3 of their 40 answers, then 5, mostly Claude Academy pages. AI Overviews linked none in the second run.

## What the cited pages say that claude.com doesn't

Zapier was cited in 10 of the 60 answers in each run, and Google's AI Overview for "chatgpt alternative" cites [its "The 4 best ChatGPT alternatives in 2026"](https://zapier.com/blog/chatgpt-alternatives/). It opens with a list:

- "Claude for professionals"
- "Google Gemini for Google integrations"
- "Microsoft Copilot for an integrated experience"
- "Perplexity for searching the web"

Then a table with "Best for" and "Pricing" for each, then pros, cons and a price line ("Free; Pro is $17/month (billed annually)"), and a note that it was "last reviewed in September 2026". An answer engine can lift any one of those lines and it still makes sense on its own.

Claude's own pages describe the product in brand lines. The homepage heading is "Question what's next", and its title calls Claude "The AI for problem solvers". The product overview's heading is "Give Claude more".

### 1. claude.com sends its title and canonical after the head on some pages

The canonical tag tells Google which address is the main copy, and hreflang tells it which language copy to show where. [Google reads both only in the head](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls). On claude.com/pricing, requested as Googlebot or a browser, the head closes at byte 5,017. The title, canonical and all eight hreflang links arrive at byte 1,319,147, inside the body. They're still in the body after the page renders in Chrome.

<BeforeAfter
  caption="Where the head tags sit in the HTML of claude.com/pricing, as Googlebot gets it today (6 of 6 requests on 6 November 2026), and where they belong."
  before={{ label: "Today", html: beforeHead }}
  after={{ label: "After the fix", html: afterHead }}
/>

It depends on the page, the user agent and the request. Of six requests each, the homepage put the tags in the body on some and in the head on others, for Googlebot and a browser alike. On the pricing page, only Bingbot got them in the head. My crawl on 6 November found the pattern on 132 of 600 claude.com pages. The pricing page is the one that matters most: it's where plans are sold, in eight languages.

**The fix:** claude.com runs on Next.js, which streams metadata into the body for any user agent not on its [`htmlLimitedBots`](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/config/next-config-js/htmlLimitedBots) list. Google's crawlers are on the default list, yet Googlebot still got the body here, so I'd set `htmlLimitedBots: /.*/` and make metadata blocking for every request. The head tags then arrive in the first bytes of every response. Then confirm with URL Inspection in Search Console that the "User-declared canonical" for claude.com/pricing and claude.com/ja/pricing is the page itself.

### 4. claude.com and anthropic.com have no llms.txt or Markdown copies

The docs do this well. platform.claude.com, code.claude.com/docs, claude.com/docs and the help center all serve llms.txt as plain text, and docs pages have Markdown copies. code.claude.com even sends Markdown to the OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot user agents.

The pages that sell don't. claude.com/llms.txt and anthropic.com/llms.txt return 404. claude.com pages show a "Copy as markdown" button, but claude.com/pricing.md returns a 404 and a request that asks for Markdown gets HTML. Whether llms.txt itself gets read [is still disputed](https://old.reddit.com/r/GrowthHacking/comments/1uyzkbo/everyones_adding_llmstxt_for_ai_visibility_the), so I'd rank this below the head tags and the copy changes.

**The fix:** a claude.com/llms.txt that lists the product, pricing, plans, comparison and solutions pages with one line each, and Markdown copies of those pages at the same address plus .md.

### 5. No structured data says Claude is software with plans and prices

claude.com marks up WebSite, Organization and Brand on every page, and FAQPage on pricing. No page says Claude is a SoftwareApplication with plans and prices. The anthropic.com homepage has no structured data, and the crawl found 89 of 600 anthropic.com pages without any.

**The fix:** SoftwareApplication markup on the product overview and pricing pages, with an Offer per plan matching the visible prices, and Organization markup on anthropic.com that links to claude.com. Suganthan Mohanadasan's [three lives of schema markup](https://suganthan.com/blog/three-lives-of-schema-markup) explains what this does for Google and less clearly for AI answers.

### Housekeeping

- claude.ai blocks GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User and Google-Extended in robots.txt, and ChatGPT still cites its homepage. That's a fair choice for an app, and it's why the claude.com pages need to carry the answer.
- anthropic.com's context engineering article links to /effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents (the "Sonnet 4.5 launch" link), which 404s. The article itself lives at /research/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents.
- 84 of the 600 claude.com pages I crawled share a title with another page, mostly Code with Claude session pages that appear under three addresses.

## How I ran this

Everything in this teardown came from SEO Skill, run by an agent, plus a few hand checks with curl and a browser. You can run the same audit on your own site.

**1. Connect the hosted MCP server.** It's free. You sign in with Google, and there's nothing to install. In Claude Code:

```sh
claude mcp add --transport http seoskill https://seoskill.dev/mcp
```

The [MCP guide](/docs/mcp) has the steps for other agents.

**2. Add a research key.** On your [account page](/account), save an Ahrefs or DataForSEO key. With DataForSEO, your agent can also ask ChatGPT and Google's AI answers your questions and check who they cite.

**3. Install the CLI for crawling.** The crawl runs on your machine:

```sh
npm i -g seo
seo login
```

**4. Ask your agent.** This is close to the prompt I used:

```text
Audit example.com and every host we own.
Ask ai_answers 20 buyer questions about our category
and list which answers name us and which link to us.
Find the non-brand searches where we have no page.
Crawl the main site and check that title, canonical
and hreflang are in the head for Googlebot.
Follow the 300 most-linked URLs on every old host
and count redirects that are temporary or end in 404.
Rank every fix by how close it sits to a sign-up.
```

If your brand is already the answer, the lesson from this one is cheap to apply: ask the engines your buyers' questions, count the answers that name you and the ones that link to you, and write the sentence you want repeated at the top of the page that should get the link.
