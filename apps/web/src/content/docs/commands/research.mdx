Use it when you need data from outside your own site: search volume for a keyword, who ranks for it, or what a competitor ranks for. You need a key from one of the three providers, and each request uses your credits with them. The [API keys guide](/docs/keys) shows how to add one.

## What you can ask for

| Action | Give it | What comes back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `keyword_metrics` | Up to 100 keywords | Search volume, keyword difficulty and cost per click |
| `ranked_keywords` | A domain or URL | The keywords it ranks for |
| `serp` | Up to 5 keywords | The pages that rank for each one |
| `competitors` | A domain or URL | Domains that rank for the same keywords |
| `backlinks` | A domain or URL | Referring domains |

Each request also takes a two-letter country code. The default is `us`.

## Run research

Ask your agent and name the provider you have a key for:

```text
Use Ahrefs to show the keywords competitor.com ranks for in the UK
```

The hosted MCP runs `seo_research`. In a terminal, `seo research --help` lists the options.

## Domain Rating column

Rows with a domain, and the target itself, get a free Domain Rating column. It is Domain Rating by [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com), and you do not need an Ahrefs key for it.

## Estimates, not your data

Provider numbers are estimates from their own index. They are kept apart from your Search Console data and never mixed into it. When you need to know what your own pages actually get, use the [SEO report](/docs/commands/report) or [`seo data`](/docs/commands/data).
