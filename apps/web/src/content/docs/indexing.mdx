Use it when traffic dropped and you want to know if pages broke or left Google's index.

## Find lost pages

Ask your agent:

```text
Which pages on example.com lost their clicks?
```

The hosted MCP runs `seo_index`. In a terminal, run:

```sh
seo index --site example.com
```

`--site` takes a domain, a URL or a Search Console property. Add `--start-date` and `--end-date` to change the window.

## What comes back

The command looks for pages that had at least 3 clicks in the previous 28 days and kept 20% or less of them in the last 28 days. Then it checks them:

- The top 20 pages are fetched live, so you see pages that now return an error, redirect, or block indexing.
- The top 10 pages are checked with Search Console URL Inspection, so you see pages Google no longer has in its index.

A page that lost clicks but looks fine on both checks may have lost rankings rather than broken. Use [`seo page`](/docs/page) to look at its queries.
