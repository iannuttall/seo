Use it when you need data from outside your own site: search volume for a keyword, who ranks for it, or what a competitor ranks for. You need a key from DataForSEO or Ahrefs, and each request uses your credits with them. For the hosted MCP, the [API keys guide](/docs/keys) shows how to add one. For the CLI, connect a provider once:

```sh
seo providers dataforseo connect
seo providers ahrefs connect
```

## What you can ask for

| Action | Give it | What comes back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `keyword_metrics` | Up to 100 keywords | Search volume, keyword difficulty and cost per click |
| `ranked_keywords` | A domain or URL | The keywords it ranks for |
| `serp` | Up to 5 keywords | The pages that rank for each one |
| `competitors` | A domain or URL | Domains that rank for the same keywords |
| `backlinks` | A domain or URL | Referring domains |
| `link_gap` | Your domain and 2 to 5 competitors | Sites that link to two or more competitors and not to you |
| `ai_answers` | Any site (DataForSEO only) | Whether AI answers cite or mention the site, for its own AI-style questions or up to 10 of yours |

Each request also takes a two-letter country code. The default is `us`. Every DataForSEO result says what the request cost, from DataForSEO's own numbers.

## Run research

Ask your agent and name the provider you have a key for:

```text
Use Ahrefs to show the keywords competitor.com ranks for in the UK
```

The hosted MCP runs `seo_research`. In a terminal, put the action first:

```sh
seo research keyword_metrics --keywords "seo audit,seo report" --country us
seo research ranked_keywords --provider ahrefs --target competitor.com --country gb
```

Without `--provider`, the CLI uses the first provider with a saved key.

## Find a link gap

`link_gap` lists the sites that link to at least two of your competitors and not to you, with the highest authority first. These are the sites most likely to link to you too.

```sh
seo research link_gap --provider ahrefs --target example.com --competitors example.org,example.net
```

It reads the top 1,000 referring domains of each site, one `backlinks` request per site. When a site has more than that, the list stops at the lowest authority it read and says so, rather than guessing below it.

## When a result is empty

An empty result always says why. A `serp` with no results means Google showed an empty page twice, which happens for rare or filtered searches and doesn't mean nothing ranks. Keywords with no metrics are listed as having no data, and their volume is unknown, not zero. DataForSEO returns many SERP results as Google redirect links. Those rows keep the domain and a shortened `url_hint`, and the notes count them. Use Ahrefs when you need the exact pages.

## Check whether AI answers cite your site

`ai_answers` starts from questions people already ask about your site. It takes the top 10 chat-style and long questions from the [AI queries section](/docs/report) of your Search Console data, so you need Google connected as well as a DataForSEO key.

```sh
seo research ai_answers --target example.com --country gb
```

To ask your own questions, pass up to 10 in `--keywords`. Then the target can be any site, such as a competitor's, and Google doesn't need to be connected:

```sh
seo research ai_answers --target example.org --keywords "what is the best crm for a small team,how do I import contacts"
```

Each question goes to ChatGPT search, Google AI Mode and Google AI Overviews. Every row says whether the answer cited a page on your site, mentioned it by name, did neither, or showed no AI answer at all. ChatGPT rows also name the model DataForSEO reports for the answer. A run of 10 questions costs about 7 cents of DataForSEO credit, and the result shows what it actually cost. Each row also lists the other sites the answer cites, and the notes rank the sites cited most.

Answers come from DataForSEO's queue and usually take about 15 seconds. Through the MCP server, any answers not ready after about 40 seconds come back as tasks that are already paid for. Ask again with the same target and those tasks to read them at no cost. The CLI waits up to four minutes, and prints the same tasks if any are still waiting; pass them with `--tasks`.

Treat each row as one sample. Ask the same question tomorrow and the answer can change, so a "neither" today is not proof you will never be cited.

## Domain Rating column

Rows with a domain, and the target itself, get a free Domain Rating column. It is Domain Rating by [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com), and you do not need an Ahrefs key for it.

## Estimates, not your data

Provider numbers are estimates from their own index. They are kept apart from your Search Console data and never mixed into it. When you need to know what your own pages actually get, use the [SEO report](/docs/report) or [`seo data`](/docs/data).
