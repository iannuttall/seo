## The hosted MCP

| What | What happens to it |
| --- | --- |
| Your Search Console and analytics data | Fetched for each request and returned to your agent. Not saved, except for sites in the weekly audit (below). |
| Your Google sign-ins | The email and tokens for each Google account you link, encrypted. Used only to fetch your own data when you ask. |
| API keys | Encrypted if you save them on your [account page](https://seoskill.dev/account). A key sent as a header is used for that request only and never saved. |
| Reports, site lists and request logs | Not kept. Request logs are turned off. |
| How you signed up | Written once: the sign-in path, the agent or app, and the site that sent you. No IP address or email. |
| Sites in the weekly audit | The last two crawls, Google's index check for each known page with its clicks and impressions, the issues list, alerts from the last 30 days and the weekly competitor check. Deleted when you remove the site. |
| Internal linking tool runs | When you run it on the website, the crawl is deleted once the result is ready, and the result after 24 hours. Page text goes to TypeSafe so Jev can judge links. |
| Payments | Stripe keeps your card. The server keeps only your Stripe ids and how many sites you pay for. |
| Pages checked live | Fetched for the request and not kept. |
| Usage counts | An anonymous count of each run. See below. |

The store lives in Cloudflare D1, and every token and key is encrypted with a server key before it is written. Your first Google sign-in is your account, so there is no password. The token your agent keeps holds only a random person id.

## Delete what the hosted MCP keeps

Sign in on your [account page](https://seoskill.dev/account) and choose **Delete everything**. It deletes your linked accounts and keys, your audited sites with their crawls, and cancels a paid plan, and it revokes the Google sign-ins (your agents will ask you to sign in again). You can also revoke SEO Skill in your [Google account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections), which stops the stored Google tokens working.

## The CLI

The CLI runs on your machine. Your Google tokens, Bing key, settings and cached responses are files on your computer, and they stay there. Reports and crawls are not saved, except the issues list you ask for. Nothing is sent to SEO Skill except the anonymous usage counts below.

## Anonymous usage counts

Both the hosted MCP and the CLI send small anonymous events, such as "a report ran". An event holds the event name, the report, the agent, the version and the week. The CLI also sends the operating system, chip type and Node version. An event never holds a site, URL, query, key, account or any report data.

The totals are public on the [usage stats page](/stats), and the [telemetry page](/telemetry) lists every field. In the CLI you can turn it off with `seo telemetry disable`.

## Who else sees your data

- **Google** answers the Search Console, Google Analytics and URL Inspection requests.
- **Bing** answers the Bing Webmaster Tools requests from the CLI, if you connect Bing there.
- **The provider you choose** gets your research request and bills your account.
- **Ahrefs** gets the domains in research rows for the free Domain Rating column.
- **Clicky** answers the Clicky requests for the sites you save.
- **DataForSEO, TypeSafe and Ahrefs** get the site's domain, its competitors, candidate keywords and, for TypeSafe, its top 25 Search Console queries, for the weekly competitor check on paid sites.
- **Stripe** takes the payment for the weekly site audit.
- **PostShiba** sends alert and weekly summary email to your Google address.
- **Your AI agent** sends the results to its model provider, under that provider's terms.
- **Cloudflare** runs the hosted server and this website, stores the encrypted sign-ins and keys and the weekly audit's data, crawls pages in a browser for sites that need JavaScript, and handles the network requests.
